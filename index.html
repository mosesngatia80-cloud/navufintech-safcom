Business operations

How we operate

NAVUFINTECH SYSTEMS operates as a technology and software business. We develop and maintain digital platforms, onboard businesses to supported software services and provide technology integrations that help merchants manage commerce activities.

Our activities include

  • Software development, maintenance and support.
  • Merchant onboarding and configuration of business tools.
  • Integration with third-party payment service providers and payment channels.
  • Digital transaction recording, reporting and reconciliation support.
  • Tools for online selling and controlled delivery of eligible digital content.
Payment services and M-PESA transactions are handled through the relevant authorised payment provider arrangements and applicable provider requirements. NAVUFINTECH SYSTEMS presents itself as a technology/software business and does not claim to be a bank or a licensed financial institution.
